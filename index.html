    Key conversion (1K–18K)

    LN converter
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
        
    Share of notes with short / long gaps to the next note in their column that become LNs, and how much of the gap they fill. Releases snap to the grid, keep a safe gap before the next note and never hold through fast same-hand runs (except inverse).
